# Human review sequence

1. Confirm service scope and accountable owner using approved organizational records.
2. Review the business impact analysis and agree recovery objectives through the organization's governance process.
3. Validate dependencies, backup evidence, and exercise history with the responsible teams; the program does not validate these items.
4. Use the report only as a checklist of missing or aging information. Record a named reviewer and disposition outside this demo.
5. Plan and authorize any tabletop or technical recovery exercise under local change, safety, and communications controls.
6. Record actual exercise evidence and corrective actions in approved systems, with owners and due dates.

The script performs none of these operational steps and cannot establish recoverability or compliance.
