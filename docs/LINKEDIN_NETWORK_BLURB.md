What can a checklist tell us about recovery readiness, and where does human evidence review still matter? This article uses a small, deterministic demo with five fictional IT service records to show how missing owners, unconfirmed dependencies, backup-evidence gaps, and aging exercise dates can become review prompts. The tool cannot prove recoverability or test a live service.
